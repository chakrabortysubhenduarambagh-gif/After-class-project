Indian Cuisine

Indian Cuisine is a diverse and rich culinary tradition characterized by aromatic spices, regional variety and deep historical roots. Indian foods changes dramatically from one state to another, shapedby local crops, weather, history and religious customs. It features both extensive vegetarians options rooted in traditions like Buddhism and Jainism and flavoful meat and fish dishes enjoyed across many regions.
