PIZZA

Pizza is a italian origin dish consisting of a usually round, flattened base of leavened wheat-based dough topped with tomatoes, cheese, and often various other ingredients such as mushrooms, onions, olives, meat, etc which is then baked at a high temperature, traditionally in a wood-fired oven. A small pizza is sometimes called as pizzetta. A person who makes pizza is known as a pizzaiolo.
