Welcome to mission IAS

IAS:

  1. The full form of IAS is Indian Administrative Service
  2. Policy Implementation: Executing policies and developmental schemes designed by the government.
  3. District Administration: Managing daily affairs at the district level, including revenue collection, maintaining law and order, and overseeing developmental work.

IAS exam is held for:

  • The IAS (Indian Administrative Service) exam—officially known as the UPSC Civil Services Examination (CSE)—is held to recruit candidates for top administrative, police, diplomatic, and revenue-related government occupations.
  • The UPSC (Union Public Service Commission) conducts the Civil Services Examination (CSE). Commonly known as the "IAS Exam", this national test recruits officers for top government posts like the Indian Administrative Service (IAS), Indian Police Service (IPS), and Indian Foreign Service (IFS).
